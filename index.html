About

I'm a passionate Full Stack Developer with hands-on experience building modern, scalable web applications from the ground up. I specialize in the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React, Node.js) and work comfortably with relational databases like PostgreSQL and MySQL, giving me the flexibility to design both NoSQL and structured data architectures. I'm always exploring new technologies and best practices to keep my skills sharp and my projects ahead of the curve.
